# Prioritized roadmap

RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Capacity: 6 person-months per release.

## R1 (5.5 / 6 person-months)

| Rank | Item | RICE | Effort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F-07 One-click quote-to-invoice | 14,000 | 0.5 | Most-watched roadmap item; removes re-entry for every accepted quote. Data already exists on the quote. |
| 2 | F-01 Automated invoice reminders | 9,600 | 1 | Half of active accounts send invoices that go overdue; directly supports 'get paid faster'. Low effort using existing email/SMS sending. |
| 3 | F-10 "On my way" SMS notifications | 4,000 | 1 | Reduces no-access visits and admin calls; simple one-tap flow. |
| 4 | F-15 Automated review requests | 2,400 | 1 | Supports customer acquisition via reviews; reuses SMS sending from F-10. |
| 5 | F-06 Card-on-file payments | 2,400 | 2 | Directly grows payment volume; payment provider already supports stored cards. |
