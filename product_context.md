# Product context (fictional)

**Product:** FieldFlow, a mobile-first SaaS app for small home-service contractors
(plumbers, electricians, HVAC, landscapers) to quote, schedule, invoice, and get paid.

**Users:** about 12,000 monthly active accounts. Most are 1–3 person businesses;
about 10% run multiple crews.

**Strategy this year:**
1. Get contractors paid faster (grow payment volume and on-time payments).
2. Reduce admin time per job.
3. Improve 90-day retention of new accounts.

**Reach is measured as:** accounts affected per quarter.
**Effort is measured as:** person-months across engineering, design, and QA.
**Team capacity:** about 6 person-months per release.
